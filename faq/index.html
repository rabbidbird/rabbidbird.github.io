FAQ

Questions about the Market Changelog pilot.

Straight answers about what the product does today, how agents use it, and what the private pilot does not claim.

What is Market Changelog?

Market Changelog is a hosted competitive-intelligence layer for AI agents. It monitors approved public competitor pages, records material transitions as durable events, and exposes search and evidence retrieval through MCP.

Is Market Changelog publicly available?

No. It is currently a small private design-partner pilot with manual review and onboarding. Individuals, students, solo builders, and teams may apply with a personal or work email. The request-access form does not create an account, subscription, or payment relationship.

What pages can the pilot monitor?

The pilot is designed for exact, operator-approved public pricing, documentation, changelog, release-note, blog, and product pages. Authenticated application pages and private customer data are not accepted.

How does an agent use the service?

A compatible agent uses MCP tools to add an approved target, queue a scan, check status, search recorded changes, and retrieve the stored evidence for a stable change ID.

Does the model choose the workspace?

No. Workspace authority is derived from the verified server-side connection. Public customer workflows do not rely on a model-provided tenant or workspace identifier.

Does a baseline count as a competitor change?

No. The first successful capture establishes the known baseline. A later material transition may create a durable change event.

Does Market Changelog guarantee every change is strategically important?

No. Severity and classification help prioritize review, but an agent or human should inspect the stored evidence before drawing a strategic conclusion.

Does the pilot include OAuth, billing, or an SLA?

No. The current private pilot uses manual onboarding and revocable workspace credentials. It does not claim public OAuth account linking, self-service billing, or a service-level agreement.

How do I request access or privacy support?

Use the request-access form for pilot consideration. Existing pilot support is available at pilot@marketchangelog.com, and privacy or deletion requests go to privacy@marketchangelog.com.
