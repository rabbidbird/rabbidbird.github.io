Privacy notice.

Effective August 30, 2026 · Notice version 2026-08-30.1

Market Changelog, operating the Market Changelog private pilot, is the data controller for this request-access form. This notice covers only the form and does not describe a public account, subscription, billing, or payment service.

What we collect

We require an email address, what the applicant wants their agent to watch, and the recurring question the agent should be able to answer later. A name, company or project name, company or project website, and MCP client are optional. Personal email addresses are accepted. Do not include confidential information or sensitive personal information in the free-text fields. We record an opaque request ID, creation, update, and consent timestamps, the privacy-notice version presented with the form, the contact-consent version accepted, and a submission count.

How we use it

We use the information only to review pilot fit, prevent duplicate requests, contact you individually about the private pilot, and securely operate the site. We do not sell it, share it for targeted advertising, send it to a CRM, use it for unrelated newsletters or bulk marketing, make automated eligibility decisions, or use the form to create an account, subscription, or payment relationship.

Infrastructure, correspondence, and logs

Cloudflare delivers and protects the site and may process IP addresses, request headers, security signals, and strictly necessary security cookies under its own terms. DigitalOcean hosts the origin and recovery infrastructure. Google Workspace routes email sent to or from the privacy address and provides the Gmail mailbox used to receive, store, and send that correspondence; message content and related mail metadata are processed there under Google's terms. The request is stored in a dedicated waitlist database, separate from pilot customer data and credentials. The Market Changelog application does not set its own cookies, run analytics, or retain request bodies, visitor IP addresses, user-agent strings, or referrers in application or origin access logs.

Retention

We delete a request from the active waitlist 90 days after its most recent submission. Encrypted or access-controlled recovery copies rotate within 14 additional days. A new submission for the same normalized email refreshes the active-retention date.

Your request

To ask what we hold, correct it, withdraw your request, or request deletion, email privacy@marketchangelog.com. We will verify the request before changing or disclosing a record.
