How it works02 / Method
  1. 01

    Choose exact public pages.

    We review a small set of pricing, product, documentation, changelog, release-note, or blog pages with each person or team in the pilot.

  2. 02

    Keep a durable chronology.

    Scheduled workers establish baselines, detect material transitions, and preserve evidence through restarts.

  3. 03

    Let agents retrieve the record.

    Agents search changes, inspect a stable event, and separate stored evidence from their own interpretation.
